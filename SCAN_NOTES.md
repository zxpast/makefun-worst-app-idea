## Why MoldWatch wins the pick

- Twist in the final words: "because neglect deserves a community".

  card), each beat more absurd than the last.
- Sounds like a real pitch: product name + "premium app for busy
  homeowners" (criterion 3: who it is for).
- 211/240 chars — uses the cap well (criterion 4), one sentence, one
  idea, no padding, no second idea.
- Vivid picture (criterion 5): a mold colony named after a relative
  getting a birthday text.
- Nothing explained; no cliché; no quote needed (quote would have
  crowded the single sentence).
